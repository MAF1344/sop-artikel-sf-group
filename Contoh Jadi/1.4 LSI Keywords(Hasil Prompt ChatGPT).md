**Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** is the practice of optimizing content so that generative AI and AI-powered search systems can **discover, understand, retrieve, summarize, cite, or mention that content in generated answers**. It is related to SEO, but the target is different: traditional SEO primarily focuses on visibility in search-result rankings, while GEO focuses on visibility **inside AI-generated responses**. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

The term was introduced in the 2023 research paper *GEO: Generative Engine Optimization*, which studied methods for improving content visibility in generative-engine responses. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735?utm_source=chatgpt.com))
