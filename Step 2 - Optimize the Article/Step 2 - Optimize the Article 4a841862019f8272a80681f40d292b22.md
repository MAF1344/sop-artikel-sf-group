### **Improving Title and Meta Description [Mengembangkan Title dan Meta Desc]**

**Tujuan:**

Mengoptimalkan **title** dan **meta description** agar lebih menarik dan sesuai topic keyword.

---

### **Mengembangkan Title dan Meta description**

1. Buka [](https://chat.openai.com/)[chatgpt.com](http://chatgpt.com/) atau [deepseek.com](http://deepseek.com).
2. Gunakan prompt berikut:
    
    <aside>
    👉🏻
    
    **[title article kamu]**
    
    Create 5 options of title and meta description.
    
    For title, it should be less than 60 characters, 
    
    For meta description, it should be less than 160 chars.
    
    Please cater to keyword **[isi topic keyword di sini] naturally**, and make sure to make it engaging and clickworthy. Use Medium style if possible.
    
    Avoid these AI words: Master, Unlock, Delve, In the Landscape Of, Realm, Tapestry
    
    </aside>
    
3. Pilih satu judul dan meta description yang paling ok.

---

**Output:**

Title dan meta description-mu sekarang sudah optimized

### **Langkah 3 — Minimize Fluff**

1. Upload dokumen Word atau copy-paste keseluruhan konten ke ChatGPT.
2. Jalankan Prompt #1:

<aside>
👉🏻

Please rate the article on below parameters

Avoiding fluffy content:

- No flowery words
- Just use simple, easy to understand language
- Only contain relevant parts.
- sentence structure
- long word difficulty
- semantically connected entities

Then please rate it from 1-10. How well does it do to avoid fluff content?

</aside>

1. Jika skor “no fluff content” di atas 7, aman, kamu bisa lanjutkan cek spacing.
2. Jika skor sama dengan atau dibawah 7, jalankan Prompt #2 ini:

<aside>
👉🏻

Analyze the text below. Identify the sentences that are most likely to be challenging for a search engine’s NLU transformer model to parse and understand. Particularly, focus on sentence structure, long word difficulty, semantically connect entities that make it difficult to provide a better answer. After each issue, give a reason and provide an alternative sentence. The alternative sentence should not contain words with more than three syllables. It must keep words that are domain specific and jargon: [PARAGRAF YANG PERLU DITULIS ULANG]

</aside>

### **Langkah 4 — Fact Checking**

Tujuan: mengecek klaim, statistik, dan data. Ini memperkuat kredibilitas artikel.

💡 Tools yang direkomendasikan:

- [perplexity.ai](http://perplexity.ai/)
- [gemini.google.com](http://gemini.google.com/)
- Google Pro Preview 3.0 di [aistudio.google.com](http://aistudio.google.com/) (aktifkan google search grounding)

Cara melakukan fact check:

1. Buka salah satu tools
2. Jalankan prompt berikut:

<aside>
👉🏻

[isi artikel keseluruhan kamu]

Please fact check above article, give reasonings, scorings out of 10 provide in table output. For second output, provide suggestions and give me which sections/sentences to change. Provide in table output.

Search the info by browsing the web. Follow the verification protocol.

**Verification Protocol (Anti-Hallucination):**

1. **Live Search Mandatory:** You must perform a fresh search.
2. **URL Validation:** Every claim MUST be followed by a live, clickable direct URL.
3. **Accuracy Check:** If you cannot find a specific recent thread, state "No recent data found" rather than inventing a link.
</aside>

1. Periksa tabel hasil, lalu perbaiki artikel kamu sesuai saran AI pada kalimat/section yang bermasalah, kecuali bagian **References**.
2. Kalau sudah, cek ulang dengan jalankan prompt ini:

<aside>
👉🏻

[isi artikel kamu]

Now please fact check above article again, give reasonings, scorings out of 10 provide in table output.

</aside>

1. Jika skor sudah di atas 8, berarti kamu good to go.

<aside>
⚠️

Jika sudah lakukan proses fact checking, **dicek ulang lagi apakah intro nya melebihi 100 words, atau ada konten yang padat.**

Kalau ada, revisi sesuai guidelines SOP utama. [Bisa refer ke checklist sebelum step 5 juga]

</aside>

**Output:**

Artikel kamu sudah punya intro menarik, gak AI-detected, mudah dibaca dan akurat datanya

### EEAT dan HCU (pakai Gemini)

**Tujuan:**

Memastikan artikel memenuhi parameter **EEAT (Expertise, Experience, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness), dan memenuhi persyaratan HCU [Google Helpful Content Update]**

---

### **Tools yang digunakan**

- Gemini

---

### **Langkah 1 — Jalankan Prompt #1 (Evaluasi Awal)**

1. Buka Gemini dan masukkan prompt berikut:

<aside>
👉🏻

*[Artikel keseluruhan kamu]*

You are tasked to create concise analysis, then provide table for these 2 analyses. Keep each parameter simple. Table only out of parameter and scoring out of 10.

Separate the table for 2 analyses.

EEAT Analysis:

You are a chief editor. You have no tolerance for fluff and your mission is to ensure that the readers enjoy reading until the end.

Act as a Google search rater and create 7 parameters of justifying content based on these questions:

1. Does the content provide original information, reporting, research or analysis?

2. Does the content provide a substantial, complete or comprehensive description of the topic?

3. Does the headline and/or page title avoid being exaggerated or shocking in nature?

4. Does the content present information in a way that makes you want to trust it, such as clear sourcing, evidence of the expertise involved, and background about the author or the site that publishes it, such as through links to an author page or a site’s About page?

5. Is this content written by an expert or enthusiast who demonstrably knows the topic well?

6. Does the content have any easily-verified factual errors?

7. Would you feel comfortable trusting this content for issues relating to your money or your life?

Then rate this content out of 10 for each of the 7 metrics.

After that, point out which parts of the article can be improved and what to improve in those parts so that it gets a better score.

Provide the output in list format.

HCU Analysis:

*You are a chief editor. You have no tolerance for fluff and your mission is to ensure that the readers enjoy reading until the end.*

*Act as a Google Helpful Content Search Rater and create parameters of justifying content attached based on these Google Helpful Content Update Inspection Checklist questions-*

*The content's topic/main keyword is:*

*Topic keyword*

*Google Helpful Content Update Inspection Checklist:*

*1. User-Centric Content:*

- *Prioritize the user's needs and interests.*
- *Provide genuine value to the reader.*
- *Answer specific questions of the target audience.*
- *Base content on real experiences or deep knowledge.*
- *Use language that target market readers understand*

*2. Originality and Depth:*

- *Ensure content is original.*
- *Provide a unique perspective or insight.*
- *Cover the topic comprehensively.*
- *Provide references to authority sources.*
- *Is it AI created content?*

*3. Clarity and Relevance:*

- *Define the main theme or purpose clearly.*
- *Provide sufficient information for the user's purpose.*
- *Avoid unnecessary or irrelevant information where short answer is suffice.*
- *Address readers' needs and issues related to the topic.*
- *Captivating introduction for readers.*

*4. SEO Best Practices:*

- *Optimize for search engines without compromising user experience.*
- *Use keywords naturally and in context.*
- *Structure content well with headings, subheadings, and bullet points.*

*5. Avoiding Search-Engine-First Content:*

- *Create content for human readers.*
- *Avoid keyword stuffing, cloaking, or deceptive techniques.*
- *Avoid thin or low-quality pages.*

*6. Feedback and Updates:*

- *Regularly update content for accuracy and relevance.*
- *Consider user feedback and comments.*
- *Monitor content performance and adjust as necessary.*

*7. Compliance with Google's Guidelines:*

- *Align with Google's general content guidelines.*
- *Avoid tactics or strategies warned against by Google.*
- *Focus on long-term value and sustainability.*

*8. Additional Checks:*

- *Ensure content is plagiarism-free.*
- *Back up claims with credible sources.*
- *Present content professionally, free from errors.*
- *Ensure readability is easy to read for 5th–8th graders.*
- *Ensure the writing follows the SPO triple semantic.*

*Then rate the content attached out of 10 for each of the metrics.*

*After that, point out which parts of the article can be improved and what to improve in those parts so that it gets a better score.*

</aside>

1. Cek hasilnya, apakah ada yang skor nya dibawah 7? Kalau tidak, maka aman. Kalau ada yang dibawah 7 maka lanjutkan ke Prompt #2 dibawah

### Converting Markdown to HTML dan Format Artikel

**Tujuan:**

---

Kalau artikel kamu masih menampilkan simbol markdown seperti #, ##, ###, berarti format heading belum dikonversi.

Contoh:

# Judul Artikel

## Subjudul

### Sub-subjudul

![](Step%202%20-%20Optimize%20the%20Article/image%2015.png)

---

### **Langkah 1 — Konversi Markdown ke HTML**

1. Salin seluruh isi artikel.
2. Buka situs [**markdowntohtml.com**](http://markdowntohtml.com/).
3. Tempelkan artikel di kolom kiri.

![](Step%202%20-%20Optimize%20the%20Article/image%2016.png)

1. Salin hasil konversi (kolom kanan).
2. Tempelkan hasilnya ke **Google Docs**. Hasilnya seperti ini

![](Step%202%20-%20Optimize%20the%20Article/image%2017.png)

---

### **Langkah 2 — Terapkan Format Standar Heading**

Setelah ditempel di Google Docs, pastikan struktur format mengikuti panduan berikut:

| **Bagian Artikel** | **Format Heading** |
| --- | --- |
| Title | **Heading 1** |
| Article Description | Normal text |
| Intro | **(Hapus)** – tidak perlu ditulis |
| Main Outlines | **Heading 2** |
| Sub-outlines (turunan dari outline utama) | **Heading 3** |
| FAQ | **Heading 2** |
| Pertanyaan FAQ | **Heading 3** |
| Conclusion | **Heading 2** |
| Konten lainnya | Normal text |
