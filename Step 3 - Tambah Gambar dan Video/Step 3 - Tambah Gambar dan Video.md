Cara Membuat Gambar AI:

Buka [ChatGPT](http://chatgpt.com/) atau [Gemini](http://gemini.google.com/).

Minta AI buatkan prompt images dulu dengan proses prompt ini:

Please create image prompts for:

Featured Image titled \[isi judul artikel\]

3 supporting Images, each with

\[isi judul outline 1, 2, dan 3\]

Please based it on Image SOP below and below guidelines/instruction.

Instruction

Determine the style based on the article context, is it better to be amateur photorealistic or vector? Give reasonings why it is better to use the style.

If it is amateur photorealistic, then it should be amateur photorealistic, dont combine each element into 1.

If it is vector, then vector, dont combine each element into 1.

Make sure the image prompts have more details to enrich the content. Jenniai Image Prompting SOP

Purpose of the Guidelines

The SOP defines how to create hero images (the main visuals at the top of blog posts) so they:

Grab attention quickly.

Visually summarize the blog’s theme.

Look clean, approachable, professional, and aligned with Jenni’s brand.

Stay consistent with a visual style that is engaging, whether through illustrations or photorealistic/vector images.

Preferred Image Style

Style: 2D flat illustration, cartoon-style, or photorealistic/vector images.

Colors: Relevant and harmonious color palette (friendly, approachable).

Composition: Clean, minimal, aesthetically balanced → no clutter.

Elements: Only include objects, people, or icons relevant to the topic.

Exclusions: Avoid distracting stock photos that clash with the brand style.

Key Takeaways

Consistency: All Jenni hero images must use 2D flat illustrations or photorealistic/vector images, relevant colors, and 16:9 ratio for uniformity across blogs.

Clarity: Each image must visually summarize the blog topic at a glance.

Relevance: Only include elements that reinforce the theme (no random icons or filler).

Simplicity: Avoid clutter, heavy detail, or unrelated imagery.

Tone: Friendly, professional, and academic — approachable but credible.

Execution: Prompts should describe:

Style + aspect ratio + colors.

Key objects/characters (robot, student, teacher, etc.).

Setting/background (classroom, desk, split-screen).

Text elements (if needed, kept short and bold).

### Tambahkan Video Youtube

Tujuan:

Menambah video YouTube supaya memberi konteks visual yang relevan dengan topik.

#### Langkah 1 — Pahami Ketentuan Video

Hindari letakkan video di paling awal artikel, karena secara visual membuat pembaca kehilangan konteks sebelum membaca isinya. Minimal letakkan 2-3 section dibawah key takeaway

Pastikan video bukan milik kompetitor. Ciri-cirinya:

Fokusnya menjual produk atau jasa tertentu.

Nama merek terlihat jelas di judul video atau thumbnail.

Deskripsi video berisi CTA ke website penjualan.

Konten video membahas topik serupa tapi dengan konteks promosi produk/jasa.

#### Langkah 2 — Tambahkan Video YouTube

Cara AI \[dengan Gemini\]

Buka [gemini.google.ai](http://gemini.google.ai/)

Chat ke perplexity langsung dengan command ini: Find me related Youtube videos about \[isi topic\], ensure to not include competitor or product links, including amazon links. No shorts allowed. This is for my site, \[isi situs jika ada\].

Pilih salah satu, yang sesuai ketentuan video di atas.
